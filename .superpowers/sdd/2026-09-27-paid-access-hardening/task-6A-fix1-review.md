2. **P1 existing paid mobile purchase with zero `ai_usage` marked ready — addressed.** The migration inserts `purchase/<Environment>:<OriginalTransactionId>/historical_paid_usage_unknown` for **every subscription already present** when it runs. The earlier device AI path did not meter paid mobile operations into `ai_usage`, so a zero-row inventory cannot prove zero historical paid consumption. The issue is retained on migration rerun; restore proof alone does not clear it. The existing four-row import control still sums day/minute counts to 5/2 exactly once across rerun and restart while retaining the historical paid gap. Genuinely free and empty installations remain ready, and a proven purchase created after migration can use its inherited free count and reserve paid usage. No historical paid count is guessed.
